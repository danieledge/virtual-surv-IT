🧠 Inferred, not confirmed. The code carries no obligation reference of its own. A same-day aggregate over one account against a hard 10000 limit is the shape of a cash-transaction reporting threshold. Two candidates, both TO-VERIFY:

AML cash-payment limit EU AMLD/AMLR cash thresholds Obliged-entity controls around large cash amounts, threshold set by regime
