# Scenario - a document input arrives as a PDF

You are a surveillance-operations analyst. You have invoked the team with:

"A vendor sent us their alert-threshold note as a PDF - it's at `inputs/vendor-threshold-note.pdf`
in this project. Everything in it is synthetic demo content. Please read it and give me a short
summary of the thresholds it sets (values and windows), plus anything odd you notice. Small job -
just the summary, no code."

If asked: no other data is involved, the PDF is synthetic, no execution of any code is needed,
and you want the summary as a short artifact.
